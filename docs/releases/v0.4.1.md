# LumenBox 0.4.1: a conversation is what you asked and what you were told

## What is new

**A conversation shows your requests and their answers, and folds the rest.** An agent's thread
used to draw every turn it ever ran: audits LumenBox dispatched on its own, messages between
agents, job notices, and everything that followed from them. On one installation a thread held
nearly twenty thousand entries and two of its owner's requests. Now a thread draws the turns a
request opened — you speaking, or a routine you set — and what belongs to them. Everything else is
one line where it happened, *Background activity · N turns · M steps*, which opens to what it stands
for. Nothing is deleted and no stored data changes; this applies to conversations you already have.

- A teammate's message is part of your request only when the agent had written to that teammate
  while working on it; a job notice only when the job was started while working on it.
- *What you asked* at the head of a thread lists your requests and goes to each.
- The plan card appears for a plan made in your own request. While an agent works on something
  else, the page says that it is busy rather than streaming the work.
- A turn you stopped, or one that reached its limit, is a line saying so — once — with the details
  behind it, instead of the notice repeated as if the agent had said it.
- Older entries load when you ask for them; a step's details are readable instead of
  `[object Object]`.

## Upgrading

No stored data changes format.

## macOS downloads

- Apple Silicon: `LumenBox-0.4.1-arm64.dmg` or `LumenBox-0.4.1-arm64-mac.zip`
- Intel: `LumenBox-0.4.1.dmg` or `LumenBox-0.4.1-mac.zip`

These builds are **not Apple notarized**. macOS may block the first opening: right-click LumenBox
and choose *Open*, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LumenBox.app`.
`SHA256SUMS` identifies the exact published files.
