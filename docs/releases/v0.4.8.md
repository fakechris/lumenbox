# LumenBox 0.4.8: teams, sign-in and routines, made safer

A fix release, mostly for installations shared by a team. Who may see and do what now follows each person's role and boxes everywhere. Sign-in is harder to guess and easier to recover. Routines say plainly when they did not run.

## Fixes

**People and sign-in**
- Wrong sign-in codes are limited per browser and overall, so a code that lasts a week cannot be guessed. A browser that hits the limit is told how long to wait.
- Invitation codes survive a restart and are stored hashed. An invitation can carry a note and a lifetime, and a person can get their own sign-in code.
- The last admin cannot be removed, signed out or demoted, and cannot revoke the last sign-in they can still use. A sole admin can still cut off a lost device's sign-in from the browser they are in.
- Removing a person or signing them out closes the desktops they have open. So does losing access to a box, including when a personal computer is transferred to someone else.
- A token made from a sign-in acts as whoever holds that sign-in now.
- When a session ends, the page reloads into sign-in instead of failing quietly. An organization's sign-in recovers after a restart.

**Roles and boxes**
- Settings, onboarding, approval rules, connectors, Files and restarts are scoped to the person's role and to the boxes they may use.
- A viewer's composer says it is watch-only. A refused send keeps what was typed, and a role change is announced.
- A member removes only their own personal computers. A computer kept when its owner is removed belongs to the admin.
- Personal computers are listed correctly, and creating or removing one walks through clearly.

**Chat**
- A coordinator's chat shows whom it asked, whether they have reported, and opens them in one click. Its running turn reads the colleague's result it is waiting on.
- Drafts are kept per agent and per conversation while you switch between them.
- Reloading keeps your place. The first run of a template is a quiet row, and the ⋯ menu opens the agent's settings.
- A first turn that ended silent after its tools succeeded now finishes with an answer. The closing nudge respects silence, guards and the tool list.
- A rejected model key is named as the reason work is stuck.

**Routines and automations**
- *Run now* and due runs start a real turn, or say why they could not. A run whose turn never started does not pause the routine or open an issue, and a waiting webhook is told it did not run.
- The automation form says when the computer could not be reached to save.

**Models**
- A MiniMax (global) key saved by 0.4.7 or earlier keeps working. Per-installation vision and output-length overrides apply whichever provider is in use.
- Several provider compatibility problems are fixed.

**Desktops and Kubernetes**
- A desktop that fails to open says so and offers *Retry* instead of spinning forever. A desktop being reopened reloads until it is back.
- Kubernetes boxes report quota and LimitRange start failures clearly, and box status and startup errors are corrected.
- A revised deliverable can be resubmitted for review.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Data from 0.4.7 loads unchanged, and a MiniMax (global) key saved under the old name is copied to the new one on first start. Invitation codes issued by 0.4.7 do not survive the update, as before; issue new ones if needed.

## macOS downloads

- Apple Silicon: `LumenBox-0.4.8-arm64.dmg` or `LumenBox-0.4.8-arm64-mac.zip`
- Intel: `LumenBox-0.4.8.dmg` or `LumenBox-0.4.8-mac.zip`

These builds are **not Apple notarized**, so macOS may block the first opening. Either right-click LumenBox and choose *Open*, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LumenBox.app`.

`SHA256SUMS` identifies the exact published files.

## Box image

`fakechris/lumenbox:0.4.8` — linux/amd64 and linux/arm64. Once the app is running, it offers *Apply update* for the box.
