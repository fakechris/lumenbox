# LumenBox 0.3.22: the list tells you what needs you

## What is new

**Each agent's row now says where things stand.** A row shows a count of what the agent has said
since you last looked, an orange mark when it is waiting on you, and "Waiting for you: …" with
the reason in place of its title. Opening a chat, or coming back to the window, counts as having
looked. What the host writes to an agent and what teammates send it do not count as unread.

**The list is ordered by what needs you.** Agents waiting on you come first, then the ones that
moved most recently, then the rest. Teams are ordered by their most recently active member, and
agents in no team are ordered among them rather than always last. A→Z is still there as a choice.

**"Needs you" now lists every pending approval,** with *Allow once* and *Refuse* right there,
and agents paused at their spend line. Approvals come first. It refreshes with the page.

**A pending approval no longer blocks the whole agent.** Starting a goal or supervision is
refused only when a turn *in that conversation* is parked on the approval, and the message names
it. Approvals nobody is waiting on any more, or older than a day, expire on their own and can no
longer be run by a late click.

**The dock badge and two kinds of notification.** The Dock icon shows the total unread across your
agents, kept current even with the window closed. You get a notification with sound when an
agent starts waiting on you, and a silent one when a turn ends with something new to read —
never while the window has your attention, and not twice within five seconds for one agent.
Clicking a notification opens that agent.

## Install on macOS

Download the disk image for your Mac, open it, and drag LumenBox to Applications.

- `LumenBox-0.3.22-arm64.dmg`: Apple Silicon
- `LumenBox-0.3.22.dmg`: Intel

The apps are signed but **not notarized**. The first time, right-click LumenBox and choose
*Open*, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LumenBox.app`.

## The box image

`fakechris/lumenbox:0.3.22` (linux/arm64) is the same image as 0.3.19 through 0.3.21, with the
same engines: opencode 1.18.25, Claude Code 2.1.250, pi 0.85.1 and mcode 0.6.12. Nothing in the
box changed.
