# LumenBox 0.3.26: one record of you

## What is new

**One record of you, read by every agent.** Settings → Mine → About me holds what you are
responsible for, what you are pushing, what you want in the long run, what you care about, how
you work, and what you prefer, as lines you can add and retract. Every agent reads the same
record at the start of each turn, before its own notes. A preference an agent learned and the
memory judge kept becomes a line here too, marked as learned; your own lines outrank it.

**The strip above the agents is one line, always.** How many decisions wait, how many reviews,
and *Open*. The list itself lives on the board. Nothing in that column can hide the agents again.

## Install on macOS

Download the disk image for your Mac, open it, and drag LumenBox to Applications.

- `LumenBox-0.3.26-arm64.dmg`: Apple Silicon
- `LumenBox-0.3.26.dmg`: Intel

The apps are signed but **not notarized**. The first time, right-click LumenBox and choose
*Open*, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LumenBox.app`.
