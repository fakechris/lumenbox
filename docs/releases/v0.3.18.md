# LumenBox 0.3.18: what the host says is not what you say, and quieter agents

## What is new

**Messages the host writes no longer speak with your voice.** Task-board pings, job notices and
other prompts LumenBox writes to an agent used to arrive looking like messages from you, with your
authority. They now carry where they came from (for example `host:audit`), the agent reads them
as information rather than instructions from a person, and a turn opened only by such a message
is recorded as the host's. Messages you send keep their authority.

**A turn takes a bounded amount from the queue.** An agent reads at most 8 queued messages, or
24 KB of them, per turn. The rest wait, in order, for the turns that follow. A message from you
still goes ahead of background work.

**The box protects itself from runaway output and a full disk.** A job that writes more than
1 GiB of output is stopped, and its log says why. When free space on the box's work volume falls
below 512 MB or 5%, new jobs are refused with the reason, jobs that are writing to disk are
stopped, and the box's self-check says so.

**A finished goal says what it decided and what it did not check.** When an agent claims a goal
is done, it must list the decisions and assumptions it made and what its checks did not cover.
The verifier tests those decisions against the work, and the review card shows both lists above
the verdict.

**Agents push less.** Each agent may send 8 unprompted updates a day. The rest are held and
delivered once, in the chat's daily briefing or at 6 pm as one "Held updates" message. Goals
waiting for your decision always go through. Settings → Team → Pushes shows each agent's day and
sets its allowance.

**`agentbox improve` learns from real sessions.** `agentbox improve <agent> --from-sessions`
finds what went wrong in an agent's recent work (corrections, refusals, repeated calls, stopped
turns, rejected goals) and replays the worst cases as read-only checks, with references back to
the record.

## Install on macOS

Download the disk image for your Mac, open it, and drag LumenBox to Applications.

- `LumenBox-0.3.18-arm64.dmg`: Apple Silicon
- `LumenBox-0.3.18.dmg`: Intel

The apps are signed but **not notarized**. The first time, right-click LumenBox and choose
*Open*, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LumenBox.app`.

## The box image

`fakechris/lumenbox:0.3.18` (linux/arm64), with the same engines as 0.3.17: opencode 1.18.25,
Claude Code 2.1.250, pi 0.85.1 and mcode 0.6.12. The output quota and the disk check run in the
box, so open Settings → Boxes and choose **Apply update** for them to take effect.
