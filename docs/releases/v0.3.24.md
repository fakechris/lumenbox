# LumenBox 0.3.24: only a decision reaches you

## What is new

**Tasks agents ask each other for no longer wait for your review.** When an agent creates a
task and names another agent to review it, the reviewers' record settles it: a passing review
closes it, a failing one sends it back. Only tasks you asked for, or asked to review, wait for
your word. The board's stage for the others now reads "under the team's review".

**"Needs you" shows decisions only.** Every item is either a decision — your answer changes what
happens next: a consent, a question, a close proposal, a review of your own ask, a
re-authorisation, a paused agent — or a notice. Notices stay on the Tasks page and in the
activity feed. The Dock badge and notifications follow the same rule.

**Small things that were confusing.** The two parts of the Tasks page say what they are (what is
waiting on a person, above; the whole board, below); *Open* brings the card into view; the
bottom-left status says "box online" instead of the probe's word; a finished plan says "plan
done" and names itself as this conversation's current plan.

**A sweep for the leftovers.** `node scripts/review-sweep.mjs` lists the tasks that were asked
for and reviewed by agents but still sit in review from before; `--apply` closes them with a note.

## Install on macOS

Download the disk image for your Mac, open it, and drag LumenBox to Applications.

- `LumenBox-0.3.24-arm64.dmg`: Apple Silicon
- `LumenBox-0.3.24.dmg`: Intel

The apps are signed but **not notarized**. The first time, right-click LumenBox and choose
*Open*, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LumenBox.app`.
