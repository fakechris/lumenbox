# LumenBox 0.4.10: organisation onboarding, sign-in and personal boxes

An organisation installation now signs its people in through DingTalk or Feishu, makes a personal box on first sign-in, and guides the first Admin through setup from `/` and `/bootstrap`.

## What changed

**Organisation setup and sign-in**
- The first Admin opens `/` or `/bootstrap`, sees a readiness checklist for the public HTTPS address, the DingTalk or Feishu app in `agentbox-organisation-env`, and the one-use `/state/bootstrap-code`, and signs in without touching the installation token. A placeholder `REPLACE_WITH_PUBLIC_HOST` address is flagged before vendor sign-in fails, and a macOS personal desktop installation on port `7777` is distinguished from an already-initialized organisation server.
- All six pre-authentication server pages and `/login` share a self-contained light and dark card layout with a `中文 / EN` switcher and localized channel names.
- DingTalk sign-in works alongside Feishu through the same channel dispatch, reports direct, mention and group context accurately, and tells a viewer they are a viewer rather than not on the list.
- At `private` and `public` exposure levels, the server requires HTTPS in front, sets `Secure` cookies and HSTS, and refuses `?token=` in browser addresses.

**Activation, personal boxes and data**
- An Admin activates people or departments to let an organisation's members in, and a member's personal box is created on Kubernetes at their first sign-in, checked against quotas.
- `agentbox-organisation-state` keeps browser sessions signed in across rollouts.
- Each installation home now carries a format number with ordered migrations, a written stable surface baseline, and declared release engine verification.
- Personal installations support encrypted relay pairing (`Noise_NK` / `Noise_IK`), multiplexed streams, per-device revocation, wake-only push notifications, and a Mac sleep guard.

## Upgrading

Data from 0.4.9 loads and stamps its format number automatically on first start. On Kubernetes (`organisation.yaml` or `control-plane.yaml`), update both the Deployment container `image` and `AGENTBOX_IMAGE` to `fakechris/lumenbox:0.4.10`.

## macOS downloads

- Apple Silicon: `LumenBox-0.4.10-arm64.dmg` or `LumenBox-0.4.10-arm64-mac.zip`
- Intel: `LumenBox-0.4.10.dmg` or `LumenBox-0.4.10-mac.zip`

These builds are **not Apple notarized**, so macOS may block the first opening. Either right-click LumenBox and choose *Open*, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LumenBox.app`.

`SHA256SUMS` identifies the exact published files.

## Box image

`fakechris/lumenbox:0.4.10` — linux/amd64 and linux/arm64. Once the app is running, it offers *Apply update* for the box.
