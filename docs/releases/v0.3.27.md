# LumenBox 0.3.27: one list on the Tasks page

## What is new

**What waits on you is on the task's own row, first.** The Tasks page no longer has a second
list above the board saying the same thing. A task that needs your word carries the mark on its
row, with the buttons to act, and those rows come first: a decision, then reviews by nearest
deadline, then the ones that have gone quiet. Only what has no task row, a consent, a question,
a paused agent, stays in the small panel above, and that panel is gone when there is nothing.

**Open shows the whole card.** Opening a task from the sidebar scrolls its card to the top of
the board, and the activity feed shrinks to a strip while the board is showing.

## Install on macOS

Download the disk image for your Mac, open it, and drag LumenBox to Applications.

- `LumenBox-0.3.27-arm64.dmg`: Apple Silicon
- `LumenBox-0.3.27.dmg`: Intel

The apps are signed but **not notarized**. The first time, right-click LumenBox and choose
*Open*, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LumenBox.app`.
