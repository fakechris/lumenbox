# Reserving a table

Turn a chosen restaurant into a confirmed reservation on OpenTable, Resy, Tock, TheFork, Yelp, the restaurant's own site or a local equivalent. The request already allows you to look; the one yes you owe is before the reservation is placed, because it commits the person and may leave a card on file against not turning up. If they have not picked a place and want advice, use `restaurant-picks.md` first.

## Kinds of request

- Party and night, no restaurant ("table for four Saturday").
- Everything exact ("Lazy Bear, two, 7pm on the 14th").
- Availability only ("anything at Frenchette Friday?"); it may turn into a booking.
- A reservation link they pasted: open exactly that.

## Settle first

- Restaurant, date, time or window, party size, notes (outdoor, high chair, birthday), and whose name, phone and email. Use the conversation and `Recall` (usual party size, seating preferences) before asking.
- Turn relative dates into weekday plus date and say both before typing. Booking pages rarely show the weekday, so a wrong date silently shows the wrong day.
- Confirm how the place takes bookings. Do not assume a platform from the brand: `WebSearch` the name plus "reservations", or `WebFetch` its site and follow its booking link. If the person named a platform, use it. Use a connector if one is listed for it.

## Steps

1. Look without booking: open the platform search with date, time and party in the URL where possible, or the restaurant's reservation page. Note every open slot in the window, seating type, deposit or cancellation terms. Playbooks: `opentable`, `resy`.
2. If a hand-built URL errors or comes back blank, reload that same URL once. Still failing: follow the site's playbook; with no playbook, use the site's own search form. Do not try other URL patterns.
3. No slot in the window: check nearby times, the next day if allowed, and any waitlist or notify option. Offer real alternatives, including a similar restaurant. Never book a different restaurant unasked.
4. Present slots and ask with `AskUser`, the options being slots you actually saw. Skip the question only when restaurant, time and party were all exact and that slot is open.
5. Book: pick the slot, fill guest details, put special requests in the notes, confirm. Card or deposit per SKILL.md.
6. Report: confirmation number, restaurant, weekday and date, time in the person's zone, party, cancellation terms.
7. Offer once to add it to their calendar, if a calendar connector is listed.

## Presenting

- Open search: three to five places with name, cuisine, area, one sign of quality, open times, verified booking link.
- Several slots at one place: list them plainly ("6:45, 7:15, 8:30, all indoors") and ask.
- Refinements ("Italian instead", "later", "cheaper") produce a new set and a new question.
- If the site is known to block you, say so briefly. If blocked, send the verified link, or check the restaurant's site for one other real route and ask before using it. Never guess that a route exists.

## Watch for

- Some places book only on their own site or by phone: give that route.
- Parties above the online limit need a large-party form or a call; do not split the group across two tables unless asked.
- Deposits, set menus paid ahead, and cancellation cut-offs go before the question and into the report.
- Book one table, not several "to be safe".
- No number on the confirmation page: say so; a connected mail service may hold the email.
- Sites show the restaurant's local time; label times for a travelling person.
