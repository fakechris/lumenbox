# Places to stay

Find, compare and book hotels and rentals, then pass on check-in details. Prices, ratings and availability come from a named site or service.

## Kinds of request

- Place and rough dates ("somewhere in Lisbon, first week of June").
- A fixed search with a budget ("two bedrooms, Joshua Tree, Fri–Sun, under $400 a night").
- A pasted listing plus dates: an availability check that may turn into a booking. Open that URL.
- A forwarded confirmation: look up, change, or fetch check-in details. Not a new search.

## Settle first

Area, check-in and check-out, guests (children's ages too), budget per night or total, and hard needs: entire home versus a private room, pets allowed, step-free access, parking, a kitchen, flexible cancellation. Default to two adults and say so. `Recall` the home city, usual party, loyalty programmes and past stays.

## Search

1. Use a lodging connector if one is listed. Otherwise rentals on Airbnb, hotels on Expedia, or the property's own site, with place, dates, guests and price cap in the URL. Playbooks: `airbnb`, `expedia`. Looking is read-only.
2. Capture: title, area, entire home or private room, bedroom and bathroom count, rating with number of reviews, price and whether fees are in it, cancellation policy, URL.
3. "Nearer the beach", "real kitchen", "free cancellation", "cheaper" are filters, not new questions.
4. Open the best few and price the precise stay (these dates, these guests) including cleaning fee, service fee and tax. Read too: cancellation terms, minimum nights, arrival hours, house rules. Search cards often show a nightly or pre-tax price.
5. Nothing found? Widen the area or the dates one step and say what changed before reporting that.

## Present

Three to five options, each with: name, area, type, rooms, rating with count, the full stay total with fees, cancellation policy. State the trade-off in money ("$120 more for the stay to walk to the venue"). With exact criteria and a clear winner you may choose, but read it back.

## Book

- Book where you found it unless they prefer elsewhere; warn if payment moves to the property's own site (another sign-in).
- Guest names, email and phone from what you know; ask for the rest. Messages to the host and ID or selfie checks are the person's: hand over.
- Insurance, damage protection and extras: offer, do not add.
- Read back listing, both dates with weekdays, guests, cancellation deadline and the checkout total. A request to book commits them too: the host can accept and charge.
- Report: confirmation code, listing, dates with weekdays, guests, cancellation deadline, total.

## Watch for

- With Instant Book the stay is confirmed immediately; with Request to Book the host decides, may refuse, and can take a day. Say which, and never call a request confirmed.
- Strict or non-refundable rates change the choice; put the deadline in the read-back and the report.
- Minimum stays and blocked nights make a card look free and checkout fail; price the exact dates.
- Arrival hours, fees for arriving late, and door codes sent on the day belong in the report. If the code comes later, say when the site says it will arrive.
- The map pin is approximate until booking; do not promise a street from it.
